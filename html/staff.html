Museum Staff

Dr. Amara Okoye — Archaeologist

Expertise: Material Culture & Settlement Analysis

Dr. Amara Okoye earned her PhD in Archaeology from the University of Oxford, specializing in ancient trade networks and material culture. She has led excavation teams across North Africa and the Eastern Mediterranean, focusing on pottery typologies and domestic structures. She uses drones, GIS, and 3D scanning technologies to document sites. Meticulous and ethical, she balances precision with curiosity. Amara leads archaeological research and curates artifact-based exhibits for the museum.

Special Considerations: International fellowship recipient; fluent in Arabic and French; ethical excavation advocate.

Dr. Lucas Reyes — Physical Anthropologist

Expertise: Human Remains & Burial Analysis

Dr. Lucas Reyes holds a PhD in Biological Anthropology from UC Berkeley. He specializes in skeletal analysis, isotopic testing, and forensic reconstruction. Using CT scans and AI-assisted tools, he studies migration and health patterns. Calm and analytical, he emphasizes ethical stewardship. Lucas oversees the study of human remains in the museum’s care.

Special Considerations: Descendant-community advocate; ethics educator; interdisciplinary collaborator.

Elena Marković — Cultural Interpreter

Discipline: Cultural Historian & Symbolic Analyst

Elena Marković holds an MA in Cultural History and Museum Studies from the University of Amsterdam. She interprets artifacts using semiotics, post-colonial theory, and indigenous storytelling. Elena collaborates across disciplines to build immersive exhibits. She challenges traditional narratives and centers marginalized voices. Her work transforms evidence into cultural understanding.

Special Considerations: Decolonial museology advocate; narrative-driven exhibit designer.
